# Retail Sales Analysis

End-to-end analysis of an online retail dataset in Python (pandas, Jupyter/Colab), from raw data to business recommendations.

## What is here

- **Retail-Sales-Analysis.ipynb** - the finished analysis (43 cells, all executed, 6 charts): data-quality checks (nulls, duplicates, ranges, types), verification of 14,526 cleaned transactions, KPIs (total revenue, total quantity, average unit price), and answers to five business questions on best/worst sellers, peak months, price thresholds and top countries. Findings: best sellers are not the top revenue makers, sales peak September-November, shoppers rarely buy above $20, the UK dominates sales, and the Netherlands holds bulk buyers - with recommendations on stock, pricing and expansion.
- **Retail-Data-Preparation.ipynb** - the preparation stage on the same dataset: understanding the data (20,000 rows, 8 columns), outlier observations, and cleaning (dropping duplicates and nulls, fixing types, filtering nonsensical values).

## Tools

Python, pandas, Jupyter/Colab.
